Services

From first sketch to finished wall

Three ways to work with the studio. Each one starts with a site visit and a conversation about how you live.

Architectural design

New houses, villas, and extensions designed for their climate zone.

You receive a complete set of plans, sections, and details, a written climate strategy that explains every decision, and a materials schedule a contractor can price.

  • Site and sun-path analysis
  • Concept, developed design, and construction drawings
  • Shading calculated for your latitude
  • Ventilation paths and thermal-mass placement
  • Permit documentation

Execution supervision

The climate strategy has to survive the building site.

A brise-soleil built ten centimetres too short, or a vent bricked up because it looked odd, can undo a design. We follow the build with contractors we trust and check the details that matter.

  • Contractor briefing on the climate strategy
  • Scheduled site visits at critical stages
  • Detail verification: openings, shading, insulation, mass
  • Post-occupancy summer check

Renovation & bioclimatic consulting

Most houses can be made to breathe without being rebuilt.

We assess an existing house and propose targeted interventions ranked by cost and effect. Often the biggest gains are the cheapest ones.

  • External shading and brise-soleil retrofits
  • Light shelves and daylight redesign
  • Green facades with local succulents
  • Opening ventilation paths and stairwell stacks
  • Kitchen ergonomics: golden zones, drawers, task lighting

Process

What working together looks like

  1. Conversation

    A free first call or visit. We tell you honestly whether the approach fits your plot and budget.

  2. Proposal

    Scope, timeline, and fee in writing. No surprises later.

  3. Design

Questions

Frequently asked

Does a bioclimatic house cost more to build?

Usually not. Most of the gains come from orientation, wall thickness, opening placement, and shading, which are decisions, not products. Where we do specify extra elements such as a brise-soleil or a green roof, we cost them against the air conditioning they replace.

Do you work outside Algiers?

Yes. The studio has designed houses on the coast, in the Mitidja, and in the south. Each climate zone gets its own strategy; a house in Ghardaïa is not a house in Tipaza.

Can you improve an existing house?

Often, yes. Retrofits such as external shading, light shelves, a planted facade, or opening up a stairwell for the stack effect can change a house without rebuilding it. We start with a site visit and an honest assessment.

What do I receive at the end of the design phase?

A complete set of plans, sections, and details, a written climate strategy explaining why each decision was made, and a materials schedule that a contractor can price.

Do you also build?

We supervise execution with contractors we trust so that the climate strategy survives the building site. We do not sell real estate.
