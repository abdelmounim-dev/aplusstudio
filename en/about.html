About

Amine and the studio

A small practice with one conviction: an Algerian house should be designed for Algeria.

Why the studio exists

Amine founded A+ Studio after years of watching well-built houses fail their owners every summer. The plans were competent. The climate was ignored. Air conditioning was the default answer, and the electricity bill was the default problem.

The studio started with a question the vernacular had already answered: how did the Kasbah and the ksour stay cool? The answer was mass, shade, courtyards, and moving air. A+ Studio applies that answer with the tools of building physics, and explains it in public so that homeowners can ask better questions of any architect.

What we believe

  • Climate sets the rules. Style follows.
  • Low-tech solutions first. Machines only where physics runs out.
  • A house is for sleeping, waking, and recovering. Light and air are health.
  • Explain everything. A client who understands the strategy will protect it.
