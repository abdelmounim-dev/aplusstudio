Projects · Blida · New build · 2025

Courtyard Villa

A family house on a flat suburban plot, organised around a planted courtyard that works as the building's lung.

Location
Blida, Algeria
Type
Single-family house
Area
240 m²
Status
Completed 2025
Services
Design, execution supervision
Strategy
Courtyard stack, cross-ventilation, thermal mass, south brise-soleil

The brief

Cool without air conditioning

The clients had lived in a conventional villa that needed air conditioning from May to October. They wanted a house for three generations that would be comfortable on a summer afternoon with the machines off.

The response

The plan wraps around a courtyard planted with local succulents. Living spaces face the courtyard rather than the street. Low openings on the north side draw air across the ground floor; a stairwell on the south side rises to a high vent, so warm air leaves continuously.

External walls are thick brick with the mass placed where night air can flush it. The south facade carries a brise-soleil sized for the June sun angle at 36° latitude, so the winter sun still reaches the living room floor.

Result

On a first summer visit the interior held around 6°C below the outdoor afternoon peak with all mechanical cooling off. Figures are placeholders pending measurement.
